Flux 0.5.0 brings FluxGram — your Telegram chats in the editor, a plugin from the catalog — and Claude
in the chats you choose: it reads them and drafts replies that leave only when you send them.

## New

- **FluxGram** (a plugin in the catalog) — an unofficial Telegram client that works through the
  Telegram API. Several accounts, signed in with a QR code from your phone or with the number; only
  the chats you add, in the window on the left and a tab each. The feed has replies and quotes,
  forwards, reactions, polls, pinned messages, formatting shown as you type, @mentions and bot
  commands; photos and videos open in a viewer, voice and round messages play with speed and volume,
  stickers and GIFs move, and a panel at the field sends emoji, stickers and GIFs. Channels and bots
  are there too; links to Telegram open inside FluxGram, other links ask first when their text isn't
  their address. Chats can be hidden behind a phrase, sorted into folders and muted for a while.
- **Claude in the chats.** Turn on **Available to Claude** in a chat's menu: Claude reads the chat back
  page by page until it understands the context, searches it, and proposes a reply on a card —
  **Send**, **Edit** (the text goes to the chat's field) or **Don't send** — in every permission
  mode. **Reply with Claude** at a message or beside Send opens a session with the chat attached.
- **Plugin API 0.5**: a service for the whole app, a helper program Flux downloads and checks by
  SHA-256, pictures and QR codes, a conversation view fed by changes (messages, media — voice, video
  on macOS and Windows, animated pictures — and a field with attachments and formatting), a window on
  the left with tabs and badges, tools for Claude in Flux's MCP server with a confirmation card, and
  Claude sessions a plugin opens. Plugins built for 0.2–0.4 keep working.

## Fixed

- Some of Claude's steps showed as denied with Claude Code 2.1.292 and later.
- Pictures a window no longer shows free their video memory: playing animations doesn't grow it.
