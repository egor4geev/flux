Flux 0.6.2 shows Claude's current limits everywhere and no longer runs out of open files.

## Fixed

- **`/usage` in Flux Remote showed the limits from when Flux started.** Each window kept the limits
  its own sessions reported, and the bot got those of the first window — where Claude last answered
  right after the launch. The limits are now one for the whole app: the newest any session
  reported. The bot hears them as they change, and when they are more than a minute old, `/usage`
  makes Flux ask Claude Code again. The status bar of every window shows the same numbers. Update
  Flux Remote to 0.1.1 too: it asks Flux on every `/usage`.
- **"Too many open files" with several projects open.** macOS lets an app keep 256 files open at
  once; three projects with their Git, language servers and plugins ran out of it at launch. Flux
  now raises the limit for itself when it starts.
