Flux 0.6.1 fixes the list of sections in Settings.

## Fixed

- **Settings highlighted the wrong section.** With several plugins' pages the list on the left no
  longer fitted, its rows got squeezed, and the highlight drifted below the open section — two rows
  lower with five plugins' pages, on another plugin's page. The rows now keep their height: a list
  that doesn't fit scrolls, and the open section comes into view. Settings are also a little taller,
  so on a large window the whole list fits.
