Flux 0.6.0 brings Flux Remote — your Claude sessions from the phone, through your own Telegram bot — and
plugin API 0.6, which lets a plugin work with Claude while you're away from the computer.

## New

- **Flux Remote** (a plugin in the catalog) — make a bot with @BotFather, paste its token in the
  plugin's settings and open the link (or scan the QR code) from your phone: the bot answers only
  you. Every session gets a topic of the chat with the bot; a message outside the topics starts a new
  session in a project you choose — or from a task of your tracker, with the tracker's own Claude
  actions (Take to Work moves the task, makes its branch and hands Claude its prompt, asking nothing).
  Claude's answer comes as it is written, with **Stop**; permissions, questions and the plan come as
  buttons; a finished turn shows its files and gives its diff as a file and a rollback. Photos and
  files from the phone go to the session. «General» is the console: the sessions' states, the
  computer's sleep, the country check, and — while the Mac is locked — every session's questions.
  Commands: `/sessions`, `/rename`, `/delete`, `/mode`, `/usage`, `/settings`. Messages that came while
  Flux was closed or the computer slept wait for your word (or run, or don't — a setting). No server:
  Flux polls Telegram itself.
- **Continue in Telegram** at a session in the Claude console sends it to the bot. Flux keeps the
  computer awake while remote sessions work (and, if you choose, all the time on the power adapter).
- **Yandex Tracker** gives the bot your tasks: My Tasks, the search as Open Task finds, and your Claude
  actions (a plugin update in the catalog).
- **Plugin API 0.6**: Claude for remote work (every session of every window, its answers as they are
  written, steps, questions, the turn's end; messages, answers, interrupts, modes, renaming and
  closing, new sessions in any project, a turn's diff and rollback, the limits, the country check),
  tasks shared between plugins — a tracker gives its tasks and its own Claude actions, another plugin
  runs them in a project's window — Git calls that never ask, the id of a session a plugin opens, the
  computer's lock, sleep, wake and power on macOS and Windows. Plugins built for 0.2–0.5 keep working.

## Fixed

- Windows: moving files to the Recycle Bin could crash Flux (#21).
- A plugin's secrets no longer show in its log's request lines.
