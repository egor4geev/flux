Flux 0.2.0 brings the Claude console: every session of the project in one place, with what it is
doing right now — and Claude no longer stops when you close its tab.

## New

- **The Claude console** (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Esc</kbd>) takes the left island, like the commit window.
  "Now" lists the project's sessions with one line on what each is doing: running a command,
  reading a file, asking you something, done. Below it is the history of the folder: recent (the
  last 12 hours), this week and all, with a filter. Subscription limits are at the bottom.
- **Answer Claude from the console.** A question waits right in its row, with its options: pick one
  with a click or a digit, step through several questions, tick several answers and send. Approve a
  plan, review or roll back a finished session's changes without opening its chat.
- **Sessions live apart from their tabs.** Closing a tab doesn't stop Claude: a working session goes
  on and the console shows its progress; an idle one is unloaded and comes back with its
  conversation when you open it. **Close** (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⌫</kbd>) moves a session to the
  history; **Archive** hides it from the history (shown again with "Show archived").
- **Select and copy text in the Claude chat**: drag across answers and your own messages, double-
  and triple-click, <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>C</kbd> keeps lists, code and tables readable.
- **Write to a subagent** from its window: Claude passes your message on while the agent works.
- **Recent projects in the Dock menu**, and folders dropped on Flux's icon open as projects.

## Changed

- The Claude chat now lives only in editor tabs: the Claude window on the right is gone, and
  sessions that were in it open as tabs.
- A new Claude logo.
- Flux looks for an update every time it starts (at most once an hour), then once a day.
- A background command in the chat pulses while it runs; clicking it in the session's status brings
  its card into view.

## Fixed

- **Quitting Flux could leave its icon in the Dock.** A process started from Flux's terminal that
  outlived it (a Docker VM helper, a `nohup` job) kept Flux "running" for the Dock. Quitting now
  names such processes and offers to end them.
- A long session title no longer runs into the status in its tab's tooltip.
